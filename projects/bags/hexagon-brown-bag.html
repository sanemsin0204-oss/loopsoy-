Sunflower Hexagon Crossbody

Sunflower tones Crossbody bag 4.5mm hook

This floral hexagon crossbody combines individual sunflower-inspired hexagons into a unique statement bag. It features a detachable strap and a simple button closure, making it both functional and easy to customize with different colours.

View supplies
Pro tip

Always use the same hook size when joining your hexagons as you used while making them. Using a smaller hook for joining creates a tighter border, causing the hexagons to pull inward and lose their shape.
